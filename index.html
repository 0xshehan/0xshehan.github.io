01 — About

About me

Hi, I'm Shehan Kosala. I love technology and crypto. I teach myself everything I know, and I want to build a big career in tech, like Steve Jobs did.

My story started in 2013, when I read an article about Bitcoin. I was hooked. I studied crypto on my own and learned how trading works.

Along the way I found out that I am creative. So I started a graphic design brand called Lunarway.

Later I worked on NFT projects, and that was the start of my life as an entrepreneur. I learned how to serve people well, how to build good relationships, and how smart marketing helps a business grow.

I am curious about many things: the world economy, politics, startups, how people think, new technology, and nature. One day I want to bring all of these together in one project that helps people for many years.

To get there, I know I need to learn computer science. It is the base for new ideas and progress.

I want to make the world a better place. My path may be different from most people's, but I am hopeful about the future in business, art, politics, and protecting our planet.
